Privacy policy

This policy describes Rclone Project Backup, a personal Linux backup utility using local rclone and the Google Drive API.

Local processing and Google Drive access

The application runs on the owner's computer and processes files and file metadata locally for backup and restoration. It requests Google Drive access through OAuth so rclone can list and manage files in the owner's Drive, including uploading, downloading, updating, and deleting backup files as required by the owner's backup operations.

The permissions shown on Google's consent screen determine the access granted. Depending on the configured scope, access may extend beyond files created by this application. The application's purpose is limited to the owner's backup and restoration tasks.

Credentials and backup storage

OAuth credentials, including tokens used by rclone to access Google Drive, are stored locally in the owner's rclone configuration. Backup files are stored in the owner's Google Drive account; files restored or retained locally remain on the owner's devices. Google processes data sent to its services under its own terms and privacy policy.

Use and sharing

Google Drive data is used solely for the owner's backup and restoration operations. The application does not sell user data or use it for advertising. Files and relevant metadata are transmitted to Google Drive to perform those operations.

Retention and deletion

The owner controls retention of local files, local credentials, and Google Drive backups through their configuration and file management. Deleting this website or revoking access does not automatically delete existing backups. Google Drive's own deletion and trash behavior also applies.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the application's access in Google Account settings. Revocation prevents future authorized access with the revoked credentials; it does not remove files already stored locally or in Google Drive. Local credentials can also be removed by the owner.

This website

These static information pages contain no analytics, tracking code, external scripts, or application sign-in. GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process ordinary website request information under GitHub's own policies.
